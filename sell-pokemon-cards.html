What Vaulture Reviews

A direct path for sellers with Pokémon TCG holdings.

Vaulture considers Pokémon TCG raw singles, graded cards, sealed products, binders, bulk and larger holdings. Enquiries can cover one standout card, a personal binder, sealed products or a complete collection.

The more detail you provide, the easier it is to assess whether Vaulture can make useful next steps. Photos, quantities, grades, condition notes and a rough location in Australia all help.

Seller FAQ

Questions people ask before selling Pokémon cards.

Does Vaulture buy Pokémon card collections in Australia?

Yes. Vaulture reviews collection enquiries from sellers across Australia, including singles, binders, graded cards, sealed products, bulk and larger holdings.

Do you buy graded Pokémon cards?

Yes. Include the grading company, grade, cert information if available and clear photos of the slab.

Do you buy sealed Pokémon TCG products?

Yes. Sealed product enquiries should include photos and notes about condition, seals, dents, tears or other packaging issues.

Can I sell a binder or childhood collection?

Yes. Binder and childhood collection enquiries are welcome. Approximate quantities, notable cards and condition notes help the review process.

Do I have to accept an offer?

No. Submitting the form does not commit you to selling. Any offer is subject to authenticity, condition and inspection.

How should I send photos?

Paste a shared link from Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, Imgur, a spreadsheet or an online collection tracker. Make sure the link is viewable.
