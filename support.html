Helpful Details

What to include when you contact support.

A few details upfront usually means fewer back-and-forth messages and a cleaner answer.

01

Your best contact details

Include your name, email and the best way to reply if it differs from the email you are using.

02

What the message is about

Mention whether it is about a collection enquiry, marketplace order, website issue or general help.

03

Relevant links or order details

If it relates to Whatnot or eBay, include the listing, order reference or marketplace username.

04

Photos or screenshots

For card condition, collection reviews or website problems, clear images help explain the issue.

Support FAQ

Quick answers before you email.

If the answer is not here, send it through to support and Vaulture can point you in the right direction.

Which email should I use?

Use support@vaulture.com.au for support, marketplace questions and general help. Use the selling form for collection enquiries when you have photos, quantities and card details ready.

Can I email photos of my collection?

Yes. You can email support, but the selling form is usually cleaner for full collection reviews because it asks for the details Vaulture needs in one place.

Who handles Whatnot or eBay checkout issues?

Marketplace checkout, payment and platform-specific order tools are handled by the relevant marketplace. Vaulture support can still help direct you if you are unsure where to start.

Does Vaulture have a walk-in address?

No public walk-in retail address is currently published. Vaulture operates online nationwide in Australia.

How quickly will Vaulture reply?

Vaulture aims to reply as soon as practical. Clear details, links and screenshots help make the first response more useful.
