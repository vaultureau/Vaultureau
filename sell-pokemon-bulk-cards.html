Bulk Guide

Sell Pokémon bulk cards with rough counts and clean grouping.

Bulk does not need perfect cataloguing, but it does need practical detail: approximate quantities, sorting level, era, condition and photos of the boxes or groups.

Submit Bulk Enquiry Packing Tips

Start with a sensible count

Bulk is easiest to review when the count is close enough to understand scale. You can estimate by rows, storage boxes, ETB boxes or stacks. Say whether the cards are mostly commons/uncommons, mixed rares, reverse holos, energies, code cards or older cards.

Sorting that helps

  • Separate bulk from obvious hits, holos, vintage cards and higher-value singles.
  • Keep heavily damaged cards separate if possible.
  • Group by era or language if the collection is already sorted that way.
  • Do not spend hours sorting if the lot is small - a few clear photos can be enough to begin.

Bulk condition still matters

Clean near-mint bulk is different from played childhood bulk. Photos of edges, corners and storage conditions help set expectations before any offer pathway.

Include bulk with the rest

Bulk can be submitted alongside singles, slabs, binders and sealed products in the same enquiry.
