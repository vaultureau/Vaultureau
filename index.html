About Vaulture

A specialist home for serious Pokémon TCG collections.

Vaulture is an Australian Pokémon TCG business built for collectors who want clear communication, a professional process and a central place to buy, sell and follow marketplace activity.

The business works with raw singles, slabs, sealed products, binders, bulk and larger holdings, with selling activity currently directed through recognised marketplaces.

What Vaulture Buys

Cards, sealed product and collections at every scale.

Raw singles

Individual Pokémon cards, from standout binder hits to higher-value singles.

Graded cards

Slabbed cards from established grading companies, with grades and cert details welcomed.

Sealed products

Booster boxes, ETBs, tins and other sealed Pokémon TCG products.

Binders

Personal binders, partial sets, childhood collections and mixed-condition holdings.

Complete collections

End-to-end collection reviews for sellers looking for a single direct path forward.

Bulk and larger lots

Bulk cards and wider holdings where approximate counts and photos can help assessment.

Selling Pokémon Cards

A dedicated selling guide for Australian collectors.

If you are comparing options for selling Pokémon cards in Australia, Vaulture has a focused collection enquiry page with preparation tips, common seller questions and the details that help assess a binder, slab lot, sealed product holding or complete collection.

The guide explains how to share photos, what condition notes matter and how to submit a no obligation collection enquiry.
