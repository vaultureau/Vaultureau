Questions

Common Vaulture questions.

Does Vaulture buy Pokémon card collections in Australia?

Yes. Vaulture reviews Pokémon TCG collection enquiries from sellers across Australia.

What does Vaulture review?

Singles, binders, graded cards, sealed products, bulk, mixed lots and complete collections can all be included.

What photos should I send?

Send front and back photos of key cards, binder pages, slab labels, sealed product corners and group photos that show the size of the collection.

Can I sell without knowing exact values?

Yes. Clear photos, rough quantities and notable items are enough to begin a first review.

Do I have to accept an offer?

No. Submitting an enquiry does not commit you to selling or accepting any follow-up offer.

Where can I shop Vaulture listings?

Use the Vaulture shop page, eBay profile or Whatnot profile. Checkout stays on the marketplace where the item is listed.

Are buyer details shown on the website?

No. Recent activity and feedback sections avoid publishing buyer names, order IDs, addresses, prices or private account details.

Where do I get order support?

For an eBay or Whatnot purchase, start inside that marketplace so payment, shipping and order protections stay connected to the order.

What is the support email?

Email support@vaulture.com.au.

What should I include in a support message?

Include your name, the page or marketplace involved, a short description of the issue and any useful screenshots or links.

Does Vaulture have a public walk-in address?

No walk-in retail address is currently published. Vaulture operates online nationwide in Australia.

Still stuck?

Send one clear message and Vaulture can point you in the right direction.
