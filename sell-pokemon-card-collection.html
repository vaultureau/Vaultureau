Collection Guide

Sell a Pokémon card collection without sorting every card first.

Binders, childhood collections and mixed lots can be reviewed more easily when the important pages, rough quantities and condition notes are visible from the beginning.

What counts as a collection?

A collection can be a binder, a childhood box, a group of slabs, sealed products, loose singles, bulk cards or a mix of everything. The first review is usually about understanding the shape of the holding rather than pricing every card immediately.

Photos that help most

  • Binder page photos, especially pages with holos, ex, GX, V, VMAX, full art, secret rare or vintage cards.
  • Close front and back photos of standout cards.
  • Group photos of storage boxes, tins or loose card stacks.
  • Any damaged cards shown clearly so condition expectations are realistic.

What to write in the enquiry

Include approximate card counts, the eras or sets you recognise, notable cards, whether items are sleeved, and whether the collection has been searched before. If you are unsure, say that too.
