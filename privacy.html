Privacy

Privacy Notice

This launch privacy notice explains how Vaulture handles information submitted through this website. It is written as a practical summary and is not a substitute for formal legal advice.

Information You Provide

When you submit a collection enquiry or contact Vaulture, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, collection details, approximate values, condition notes, notable items and links to photos, inventory files or collection trackers.

How The Information Is Used

Vaulture uses submitted information to assess potential collection purchases, contact you about your enquiry, keep appropriate business records and help prevent spam or misuse of the website.

External Service Providers

Website hosting, form submission handling and email delivery may be provided by external services such as GitHub Pages, Formspree and Cloudflare Email Routing. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

No Payment Processing On This Website

Vaulture does not currently operate checkout or payment processing on this website. Marketplace purchases are handled by the relevant external marketplace.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@vaulture.com.au. For collection enquiries, contact sell@vaulture.com.au.
