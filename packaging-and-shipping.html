Packaging & Shipping

Package Pokémon cards so condition does not change in transit.

Good packing protects the collection, keeps the review clean and reduces avoidable damage. Do not ship anything until the next step has been agreed with Vaulture.

Start Collection Enquiry Contact Support

Before packing

  • Photograph the collection before it is packed.
  • Take close-ups of higher-value cards, slabs and sealed product damage.
  • Keep a simple list or note of what is included.
  • Confirm the next step with Vaulture before sending anything.

Raw cards

Sleeve valuable raw cards. Use toploaders, card savers or firm cardboard support for standout cards. Group protected cards in team bags or similar sleeves so they cannot slide around.

Slabs and sealed products

Wrap graded slabs so they cannot rub against each other. For sealed products, protect corners and avoid crushing pressure. Use a box with enough padding that contents do not move when gently shaken.

Shipping basics

  • Use tracked postage and keep the tracking number.
  • Avoid envelopes for anything valuable or rigid.
  • Use a sturdy box, not packaging that collapses easily.
  • Do not tape directly onto sleeves, toploaders, slabs or sealed products.

Need a review first?

Send photos and details before shipping. Vaulture can review the enquiry and explain next steps.
