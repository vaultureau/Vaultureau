Sealed Products

Sell sealed Pokémon products with condition shown clearly.

Sealed product value can depend heavily on seal integrity, box condition, demand and authenticity signals. Clear photos help avoid guessing.

Submit Sealed Product Enquiry Shipping Guide

What sealed products can include

Sealed enquiries can include booster boxes, elite trainer boxes, tins, blisters, collection boxes, sleeved boosters, build and battle kits and sealed bundles.

Photos that matter

  • Front, back, top, bottom and side photos of boxes.
  • Close-ups of seals, tears, dents, crushed corners or loose wrapping.
  • Photos of case labels or product codes if available.
  • Group photos if you have multiple sealed items.

Be upfront about damage

A small dent, tear or seal concern can materially change how sealed product is reviewed. Honest notes make the conversation faster and clearer.

Have sealed and singles together?

Submit everything through one collection enquiry and describe the sealed products separately.
