Graded Cards

Sell graded Pokémon cards with slab details ready.

Graded cards are easier to review when the grade, grading company, cert label and slab condition can be checked clearly from photos.

Submit Slab Enquiry Main Selling Guide

What to include for slabs

  • Front photo showing the card, label, grade and certification number.
  • Back photo showing the slab and card clearly.
  • Any notes about cracks, scratches, label issues or holder damage.
  • Whether the slabs are PSA, CGC, BGS, ACE or another grading company.

Condition still matters after grading

The grade is important, but slab condition, demand for the card, population, language, set, artwork and current liquidity can all affect how a graded card is reviewed.

Submitting multiple graded cards

If you have a slab lot, a simple list with card name, grade and cert number can help. Clear group photos are useful, but key cards should still have individual photos.
