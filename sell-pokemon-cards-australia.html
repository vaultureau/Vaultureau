Selling Guide

Sell Pokémon cards in Australia with clearer expectations.

If you are deciding what to do with Pokémon cards, slabs, sealed products or a wider collection, start by getting the details organised enough for a practical review.

Start Collection Enquiry Packaging Guide

What Vaulture looks for first

A useful first review usually comes down to item mix, condition, authenticity signals and how much work is needed to prepare the cards for resale. You do not need to know every price before getting in touch, but clear information helps avoid vague guessing.

  • Photos of notable cards, binder pages, slabs and sealed items.
  • Approximate counts for raw cards, bulk, graded cards and sealed products.
  • Condition notes for whitening, dents, scratches, bends, tears or seal damage.
  • Any known standout cards, sets, grades or product names.

Different cards need different detail

A single high-value card needs sharp front and back photos. A binder needs page photos and a list of standout cards. A sealed product needs photos of corners, seals and surface damage. Bulk needs rough counts and a clear idea of whether it is mostly modern commons or includes older cards and holos.

Ready to send details?

Use the collection enquiry form when you have a few photos, rough quantities and a short description. Submitting an enquiry does not commit you to selling.
